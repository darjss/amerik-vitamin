Rewrite it, with the business in the model

Nine repos and two years later, the 13 tables are still from ecom-dashboard. In that time the catalog was rebuilt from scratch four or more times, search was replaced three times, and the storefront got three looks. Variants were deleted in January 2025 and spent the next year and a half being guessed back. The new repo starts from what the agents were actually asked to do: variants, a stock ledger, payment events, dispatch, and a Telegram admin that calls real actions instead of raw SQL.

Dates are commit dates and session start times. Where an incident has no exact date in the logs, it sits at the start of the session where it came up.
